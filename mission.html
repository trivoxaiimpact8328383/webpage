01 / PURPOSE
WHY WE EXIST

Education should create more than knowledge. It should create possibility.

People need more than information. They need the confidence, tools and environments required to use what they learn.

Our mission is to reduce the distance between learning something and being able to apply it meaningfully in the real world.
