Career Signal

Career clarity starts before the job search.

Instead of beginning with job titles, begin by understanding the work you enjoy, the skills you are developing and the kind of problems you want to become capable of solving.

A stronger career decision connects interest, ability, evidence and opportunity.

Role DNA

A role is more than a job title.

Explore the capabilities that sit underneath professional roles and understand what the work actually asks you to practice.

DNA 01 Problem Thinking

Understand the requirement before jumping directly into a solution.

DNA 02 Practical Execution

Turn ideas into something that can be tested, reviewed and improved.

DNA 03 Clear Communication

Explain decisions, progress and outcomes so other people can understand your work.

DNA 04 Continuous Improvement

Use feedback and reflection to improve both the work and the way you approach it.

Career Story

Learn to explain your value.

A useful career story connects what you learned with what you built, how you improved and where you want to contribute next.

01 / CONTEXT

What were you trying to solve?

Give enough context for another person to understand why the work mattered.

02 / ACTION

What did you actually do?

Explain your contribution instead of describing only what the team did.

03 / DECISION

Why did you choose that approach?

Show the reasoning behind important technical or product decisions.

04 / RESULT

What changed because of your work?

End with the result, improvement, learning or practical outcome.
