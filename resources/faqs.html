Questions & Answers
01

What is TRIVOX AI Impact?

TRIVOX AI Impact is a learning and impact platform focused on helping students build practical skills, explore technology and participate in meaningful learning experiences.

02

What can students learn on TRIVOX?

Students can explore areas such as AI, programming, web development, app development, data science and cyber security through learning resources, programs and practical activities.

03

Does TRIVOX provide student programs and workshops?

Yes. TRIVOX can host student programs, workshops, bootcamps and learning experiences designed around practical skills and real-world participation.

04

Are TRIVOX events online or offline?

TRIVOX events may be online, offline or hybrid, depending on the event format, location and audience. Event details are provided on the Events page.

05

Can beginners use TRIVOX learning resources?

Yes. The learning resources are designed to support different levels of learners, including students who are just starting with a topic.

06

Does TRIVOX offer internships or career opportunities?

Internship, campus hiring and career opportunities may be shared through the Careers section whenever positions or programs are available.

07

Can colleges work with TRIVOX?

Yes. Colleges can collaborate with TRIVOX for learning programs, workshops, student initiatives and other educational experiences.

08

How do I get started with TRIVOX?

Start by exploring the Learning, Resources, Programs or Events sections and choose the experience that matches what you want to learn or participate in.
